02 / THE PROBLEMSmall budgets, spread in the wrong places

These were five local service businesses on modest budgets, where every wasted dollar shows. The same patterns kept appearing:

  • Search campaigns capped by budget while Performance Max ran freely on weaker results. At the rug cleaner, Search converted at 12.99% and Performance Max at 0.59%.
  • Broad keyword lists that didn't get traffic. The rug cleaner had 28 broad-match keywords, and only one received any volume.
  • "Poor" ad strength from ads recycling the same few phrases.
  • Lead forms switched off or disapproved, one of them for putting a phone number in the confirmation message.
  • Repeat clicks counted as extra leads, inflating totals.

03 / WHAT I DIDAccount by account

Business A · Sealing

Doubled Search during a lead slowdown, without raising spend

Leads slowed in June. Search was the strongest channel, so I doubled its budget and funded it by trimming two Performance Max campaigns that had a slow stretch.

$6/day→$12/dayTile & grout Search campaign
$5.75 + $5.00→$3.00 + $2.75Glass sealing and stone sealant Performance Max

Both trimmed campaigns had good lifetime costs (about $7 and $12 per conversion), so I flagged them to be restored if the slowdown proved temporary. By October, glass sealing had spent $90 with no leads, which supported keeping it lean.

Business B · Rug cleaning

Tightened match types and switched off automated broadening

  • Moved 15 keywords to exact match and 9 to phrase match, then verified the change.
  • Turned off AI Max on the Search campaign so Google couldn't widen it to loose matches.
  • Set budgets at $46/day Performance Max and $20/day Search after the review.
  • Flagged that Google Ads showed "no recent conversions" for an action that had recorded 82 in October, so the import could be checked.
Business C · Exterior cleaning

Fixed weak ads and added the missing conversion

  • Rewrote a "Poor" ad-strength roof cleaning ad with 15 new headlines and 4 descriptions, using only real business details.
  • Built new dryer vent and roof/soft wash ads and paused two roof ads sitting in the wrong ad group.
  • Added a free-estimate conversion in Tag Manager.
  • Added two of the client's own videos to Performance Max.
  • Set a $85–$115 cost-per-lead benchmark. October landed at $102.85.
  • Flagged vent cleaning Search: $394 in October with no leads.
Business D · Flooring, location 1

Showed where the leads came from and turned lead forms back on

I showed the client that its competitor-targeting campaign drove 91 of 108 October leads. I re-enabled the two "free estimate" lead forms on its main Search and Performance Max campaigns, with leads exporting automatically, and noted the competitor campaign had no lead form yet.

Business E · Flooring, location 2

Got a disapproved lead form approved and fixed the counting

  • The lead form was disapproved because its confirmation message contained a phone number. I removed it and resubmitted all four linked campaigns.
  • Changed form submissions and phone clicks to count once per click, so repeat clicks stopped inflating the total.
  • Set the main form-submit action to primary after Google flagged "needs attention".
  • Explained a spend shortfall ($1,072 against $1,500) as the client's own budget cut, not a delivery problem.
All five · Reporting

Wrote a strategy one-pager for every client

For the November review I wrote a two-page summary and strategy for each client: where things stand, work completed, strategy, 90-day goals, and what we need from them.
